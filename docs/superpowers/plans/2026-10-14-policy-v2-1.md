## Review Focus

- Missing contacts and invalid no-action outcomes must not retain charges.
- Missing/negative/conflicting historical traffic records must stay distinct.
- Safety, amount, distance and fraud/confidence boundaries must block prohibited rulings.
- The second Judge must receive its original assessment and exhausted rebuttal budget.
